# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Web3 HR managers, startup founders, and DAO contributors distributing recurring salaries and direct payments on the Stellar blockchain network.

## Product Purpose

StellarPay provides a non-custodial, decentralized payroll and salary distribution platform on Stellar Testnet. It enables administrators to manage employee rosters, perform instant direct XLM transfers, and trigger automated bulk smart contract payouts powered by Soroban.

## Positioning

Non-custodial, instant, transparent bulk salary payouts on Stellar & Soroban with zero administrative overhead.

## Brand Personality

Trustworthy, Efficient, and Modern. The interface communicates financial stability, high precision, and sleek operational clarity without over-stimulating decor.

## Anti-references

Explicitly avoid cluttered SaaS templates, low-contrast gray-on-gray text, cheesy 3D crypto illustrations, and glassmorphism overload.

## Design Principles

- **Clarity over Clutter**: Prioritize clean data hierarchy, bold typography, and unambiguous status feedback over decorative flair.
- **Instant Visual Feedback**: Provide real-time toast notifications, transaction execution logs, and network status badges for every action.
- **Financial Grade Security & Transparency**: Display exact Stroop calculations, wallet addresses, and direct Stellar Expert explorer links for auditability.
- **Responsive Operational Ease**: Ensure mobile-first layouts so HR administrators can approve payroll from any device screen size.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA compliance, text contrast ratio ≥ 4.5:1, keyboard navigation focus indicators, and `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` alternatives.
